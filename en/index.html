Who we are

Founder

A two-decade journey in education and technology

Vortex888 grew out of the journey of Gustavo Adolfo Morceli Rodrigues, an entrepreneur and author with more than two decades at the intersection of education and technology.

Before the holding existed, there was a practical question: how do you bring real educational technology to Brazilian public schools? The answer was built in stages — the textbook collection O Fantástico Mundo de ROB (The Fantastic World of ROB), for early childhood education; the ROB Kit, an unplugged-programming robot protected by Brazilian patent BR 202021026041-1; and Hexa Clima, a Brazilian-made IoT weather station now installed in twelve public schools in Cachoeiras de Macacu, Rio de Janeiro.

As CEO of PETE, he has kept educational robotics kits in domestic production for more than twenty years — today documented in an academic publication by Edusp alongside the leading imported kits used in Brazil.

The ClimAção project, developed by BDN Tecnologia, was selected by UNESCO for presentation at Digital Learning Week 2026 in Paris.

Today Vortex888 brings together the BDN Tecnologia and NeuroInsights units, holding on to its founding principle: technology developed in Brazil, for the Brazilian context, with ownership and data that stay here.
